      What gets emailed

      • Acknowledgement to the faculty member as soon as their department submits a report.
      • Verification result to the faculty member when you set a report to Verified, Returned for Correction or Rejected, with your remarks.
      • Monthly reminder at 10:00 AM on the 20th of every month to departments with no report submitted that month, and a summary to the coordinator email.

      Emails go to the faculty emails in Departments & faculty. If a faculty member has no email on record, the email comes to the coordinator email instead.
